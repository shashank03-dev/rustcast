# Code of Conduct

RustCast is a friendly project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests or
anywhere else, is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people use different distros, desktops and workflows.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Trolling, spam or deliberately derailing discussions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who break
these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
